Design + development

I'm Bhargav Borgaonkar, a game developer who works mostly in Unity and C#. I like taking a rough gameplay idea and actually getting it running — most of what I know today, I picked up by building things myself and poking around inside gameplay systems until they made sense.

I've built both 2D and 3D games, and I'm the kind of person who wants to know why something works the way it does — how movement, collisions and pacing come together to make a game feel right. Game design and level design pull me in the same way.

Right now I'm trying to get better the honest way: by building real things, breaking them, and fixing them. I'm looking for a job or internship where I can keep doing that alongside people who've been at it longer than I have.

02 — How I work

Development process

Here's how I take a project from a rough idea to a finished, playable build.

IDEA→ CORE LOOP→ PROTOTYPE→ PLAYTEST→ ITERATE→ BALANCE→ POLISH

I combine design thinking with technical implementation: understand the player experience, prototype the idea quickly, test it, identify problems, and use Unity and C# to iterate toward a better game.
